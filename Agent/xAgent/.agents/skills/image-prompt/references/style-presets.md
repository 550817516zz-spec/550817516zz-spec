# 风格预设参考

按需读取本文件。优先选择与用户主题匹配的预设，不要把多个冲突风格混在一起。

## 写实摄影

```text
photorealistic, 35mm photography, natural skin texture, realistic lighting,
subtle film grain, shallow depth of field, high detail, color graded
```

适合：人物肖像、产品图、生活方式、纪实场景。

## 电影感

```text
cinematic still, anamorphic lens, dramatic rim lighting, volumetric light,
teal and orange color grade, wide shot, atmospheric haze, film still
```

适合：故事场景、海报、情绪化画面。

## 国风 / 东方美学

```text
Chinese ink painting aesthetic, elegant negative space, silk texture,
misty mountains, traditional architecture, muted mineral pigments,
poetic atmosphere, fine brushwork
```

适合：山水、古风人物、文化主题、节日海报。

## 赛博朋克

```text
cyberpunk city, neon signs, rain-soaked streets, holographic advertisements,
purple and cyan lighting, reflective puddles, futuristic techwear,
high contrast, cinematic night scene
```

适合：未来城市、科技产品、游戏概念图。

## 动漫 / 插画

```text
anime illustration, clean line art, expressive eyes, soft cel shading,
vibrant colors, detailed background, dynamic composition, high quality
```

适合：角色设定、轻小说插画、头像。

## 3D 渲染

```text
3D render, stylized character, soft studio lighting, subsurface scattering,
clay material, octane render, high detail, clean background
```

适合：IP 形象、产品展示、盲盒风格。

## 水彩

```text
watercolor painting, wet-on-wet washes, visible paper texture,
soft edges, translucent colors, delicate brush strokes, airy composition
```

适合：治愈系插画、书籍封面、文艺海报。

## 极简海报

```text
minimalist poster design, strong geometric composition, limited color palette,
large negative space, bold typography area, flat graphic style, high contrast
```

适合：活动海报、品牌视觉、社交媒体配图。

## 通用负面提示词

```text
low quality, blurry, pixelated, deformed hands, extra fingers, bad anatomy,
bad proportions, watermark, signature, text, logo, cropped, out of frame,
oversaturated, flat lighting, duplicate subject
```

按题材删减：海报设计可能允许文字，产品图不需要人物相关负面词。
